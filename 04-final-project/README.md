# My Project

My project is a gardening simulator where you can do simple gardening actions like watering, giving sunlight and feeding the plant with fertilizer/flies.
The outcome is a growing the plant ( a venus fly trap) with also other different effects.

I used the various parts of different codes from our previous activites to take what I learnt and put it to use into this project. This not only challanges what I can remember from what I learnt but also encourages me to take these knowledege to create something that is unqiuely mine.
